---
name: spitballing
description: Gut-check a half-baked idea before the agent runs with it.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

I'm spitballing. This ask may be smart or a dumb move. I don't know which.

Start with a verdict: **good idea**, **bad idea**, or **right goal, wrong move**. Then give the strongest case against it, and a better move if you see one.

What comes next follows my wording. If I asked you to do something and it is a good idea, do it. If I only asked a question, stop after the answer. On a bad idea or a wrong move, stop and let me choose.
